## About me

I'm **Mohammad Kaif**, founder of **AtomProd** and a developer who enjoys building software from the ground up. I like working across the full product lifecycle: understanding a problem, designing the experience, engineering the solution, and refining it through real use.

My projects cover web development, desktop and mobile applications, offline-first software, automation, and tools for creative communities.

- 🚀 Building useful products and software through AtomProd.
- 🧠 Interested in full-stack engineering, cross-platform apps, local-first architecture, and automation.
- 🛠️ I enjoy taking on technically challenging ideas and turning them into usable tools.
- 🤝 Open to thoughtful collaboration, feedback, and contributions to open-source projects.

## How I like to build

- **Solve real problems.** Start with a useful outcome, not just a technology demo.
- **Keep ownership with users.** Prefer offline-friendly and local-first approaches when they make sense.
- **Be transparent.** Document setup, trade-offs, and known limitations.
- **Keep improving.** Test, learn from feedback, and iterate.
